Privacy Policy for the "VetRecord" app

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Developer: Dmitry Vanyukov ("Developer"). Contact: corvinspb@gmail.com

App: "VetRecord" (shown as "Веткарта" when the phone language is Russian or Ukrainian; package com.vetrecord.app) for Android.

In short

VetRecord works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit or sell your data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

What data the app handles

You enter information into the app yourself: pet names, breed, sex and birth date, photos, notes, vaccine and medication names, dates and schedules, vet clinic names and your reminder settings. This data:

  • is stored only in the app's private storage on your device;
  • is never sent to the Developer or to any third party;
  • is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or shared storage, and it does not hold the INTERNET permission.

Android permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show vaccine and medication reminders.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — so reminders arrive at the time you choose.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — so reminders are restored after your phone restarts.

Photos and files

You pick a pet photo yourself through the system file picker; the app stores a copy inside its own storage. CSV export saves a file to your device's Downloads folder; CSV import reads only the file you select. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.

Android backup

If Android backup (Google Drive) or device-to-device transfer is enabled on your phone, Android may store the app's data in your own Google account under Google's terms. The Developer has no access to these backups. You can turn backups off in your phone's settings.

Sharing with third parties

The Developer does not receive your data and therefore does not share, sell or disclose it. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data. It does not collect personal data at all.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a pet, a care plan or a history entry inside the app. To erase everything, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → VetRecord → Storage → Clear data. Because the Developer does not receive your data, there is nothing to request from the Developer.

Security

Your data stays in the Android app sandbox. Its safety also depends on your device's protection (screen lock, system updates). CSV files you export to Downloads are not encrypted — store and share them with care.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
